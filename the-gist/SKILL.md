---
name: the-gist
description: "Inform the user about a subject in a short and concise way."
---

The user has given a subject they want the gist of.
If the information is already in the context: Summarize.
If the information is not available in the context, gather information until you can give an answer that satisfies the matter.
Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English and use domain relevant language from agreed upon vocabulary (if available).
Avoid long and convoluted sentences.
